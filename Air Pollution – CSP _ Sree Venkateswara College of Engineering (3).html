About the Project
What we did and why it matters




The Problem
Air pollution from vehicles, industry, dust and burning of waste harms health and the environment. Many people in our locality are unaware of its causes and simple ways to reduce it.


Our Objective
To educate the community about air pollution, identify local pollution sources, and encourage practical steps such as tree plantation, carpooling and waste management.


Our Approach
Area survey, awareness campaigns, pamphlet distribution, interaction with residents and students, and plantation drives.
